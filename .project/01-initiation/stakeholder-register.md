# Business Case

## Market Opportunity
- Saudi Arabia has a thriving car culture (especially off-road, modified cars, and weekend trips)
- Growing interest in organized group activities post-vision 2030
- Existing solutions are generic (WhatsApp, Meetup) and lack Saudi-specific features

## Revenue Potential
- Freemium model: free base app, premium community features
- Transaction fees on payments
- Featured destinations / sponsored listings

## Cost Estimate (MVP Development)
- Development: ~3-4 months part-time
- Infrastructure: ~$200-500/month (scales with users)
- External services: Google Maps ($200 credit/month), payment gateway fees

## ROI
- Break-even at ~500 active communities or ~2,000 monthly trips
- Strategic value: user base for future automotive marketplace
